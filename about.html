Our Story

Crafting Dreams Since 2010

From a small workshop to a nationwide brand, DreamRest has been on a mission to revolutionize how people sleep — one handcrafted mattress at a time.

Our Mission

Better Sleep for Everyone

We believe everyone deserves a great night's sleep. Our mission is to combine cutting-edge sleep science with artisanal craftsmanship to create mattresses that don't just support your body — they transform your wellbeing.

Every material is ethically sourced, every stitch is placed with intention, and every mattress is tested for 200+ hours before it reaches your home.
