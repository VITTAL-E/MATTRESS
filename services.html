Coverage Details

Warranty & Returns

Everything you need to know about our industry-leading warranty.

What does the 15-year warranty cover?
Our warranty covers manufacturing defects including sagging greater than 1 inch, broken or protruding coils, and deterioration of foam materials that causes loss of support.
How does the 100-night trial work?
Sleep on your new mattress for up to 100 nights. If you're not completely satisfied, contact us for a free pickup and full refund. No questions asked.
Can I exchange for a different firmness?
Yes! During your 100-night trial, you can exchange your mattress for a different firmness level at no additional cost.
Is the warranty transferable?
Yes, our warranty is fully transferable to new owners if you sell or gift your mattress.
What about accidental damage?
We offer an optional AccidentGuard plan that covers spills, stains, and tears for an additional $99 for 5 years of coverage.
