Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about DreamRest.

Do you offer free shipping?
Yes! We offer free white-glove delivery and setup on all mattress orders within the continental US.
What sizes are available?
We offer Twin, Twin XL, Full, Queen, King, and California King. Custom sizes are also available.
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available for an additional fee.
Can I try before I buy?
Absolutely! Visit any of our showrooms to try every mattress. Plus, our 100-night trial lets you test at home.
Do you remove old mattresses?
Yes, our delivery team will remove and recycle your old mattress at no extra cost.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and offer 0% APR financing through Affirm.
