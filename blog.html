Quick Tips

Sleep Better Tonight

Science-backed tips from our sleep experts.

01

Keep a Consistent Schedule

Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day — even on weekends. This regulates your circadian rhythm.

02

Optimize Room Temperature

The ideal sleep temperature is 65-68°F (18-20°C). A cool room triggers natural sleep hormones.

03

Limit Screen Time

Avoid blue light from devices 1 hour before bed. Blue light suppresses melatonin production.

04

Invest in Quality Bedding

Your mattress and pillows directly impact sleep quality. Replace your mattress every 8-10 years.

05

Create a Wind-Down Ritual

Reading, light stretching, or meditation before bed signals your brain that it's time to sleep.
